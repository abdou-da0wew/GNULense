## Quick Reference

**Where to add a feature?**

- New domain? → `scopes/<domain>.<capability>/`
- Cross-scope logic? → `mergers/`
- Multi-step orchestration? → `flows/`
- Wiring only? → `strappers/`

**Rules:** scopes never import from another scope, mergers coordinate only, flows orchestrate only, connectors do I/O, managers hold business rules.
